Brazilian company-data service built on the Receita Federal CNPJ registry. Paste a business idea in plain text to see how many companies already operate in that space, plus CNPJ lookup, advanced search, geolocation, and change-monitoring. Explicitly agent-first, shipping a full stack of machine-readable discovery artifacts including OpenAPI, a hosted MCP server, and llms.txt.

**Areas:** Business & Company Data · Government open data · Receita Federal · CNPJ · Brazil · Regulatory & Compliance · KYB · Search · Data Enrichment · Geolocation · Monitoring & Alerts · Agent-Native

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages.
